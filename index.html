Matthew E. Weingarten

My research aims to maximize the performance and efficiency of computer systems through performance characterization, hardware profiling, and compiler optimizations.

My current research projects focus on the following questions:

How can we automate the generation of performance characterization hardware to enable agile hardware design?
How can we build compiler infrastructure for systems-on-chip with specialized hardware accelerators?
How can we design profiling hardware to eliminate critical bottlenecks in data center processors?

In my day-to-day work, I develop and extend systems across the RISC-V and open-source hardware and compiler ecosystems, including Chipyard, FireSim, LLVM, MLIR, and CIRCT.

I am affiliated with Columbia University’s ICE Lab and am advised by Prof. Dr. Tanvir Ahmed Khan.
